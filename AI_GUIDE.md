# Smart Student Wallet AI

# AI Guide

Version: 1.0

---

## AI Objectives

The AI module helps students:

- Predict future expenses
- Analyze spending habits
- Suggest saving strategies
- Generate financial reports
- Answer finance-related questions

---

## AI Features

### 1. AI Chat Assistant

- Answer user questions
- Explain spending patterns
- Provide financial advice

---

### 2. Expense Prediction

Predict next month's:

- Total Expense
- Most Spending Category
- Expected Savings

---

### 3. Financial Health Score

Calculate score based on:

- Budget Usage
- Savings
- Expense Frequency
- Spending Pattern

Score Range:

- 90-100 Excellent
- 70-89 Good
- 50-69 Average
- Below 50 Needs Improvement

---

### 4. Spending Analysis

Analyze:

- Highest Spending Category
- Weekly Trend
- Monthly Trend
- Daily Average

---

### 5. AI Suggestions

Examples:

- Reduce food expenses.
- Spend less on entertainment.
- Increase monthly savings.
- Stay within your budget.

---

### 6. Monthly Report

Generate:

- Total Income
- Total Expense
- Savings
- Top Category
- Financial Score
- AI Suggestions

---

### 7. Weekly Report

Generate:

- Weekly Expense
- Daily Average
- Top Spending Category
- Budget Status

---

## AI Input

- Transactions
- Budget
- Categories
- Wallet Balance

---

## AI Output

- Predictions
- Reports
- Suggestions
- Financial Score
- Chat Responses
